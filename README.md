# Optical Technology for Data Centres 01 — Pluggable Transceivers to Linear-Drive Optics

What is inside a pluggable optical module and where its power goes: form factors and MSAs, the Ethernet optical interfaces from 400G to 1.6T, host electrical lanes, the retiming DSP, and what linear-drive (LPO) and linear-receive (LRO) modules save by removing it and what they give up.

Topics: QSFP-DD / OSFP, IEEE 802.3, DSP retimer, LPO, LRO, W per port.

**Live site:** https://brendanjameslynskey.github.io/OptDC_01_Pluggables_to_LPO/

Part of the [Optical Technology for Data Centres series](https://github.com/BrendanJamesLynskey/Optical_Hub_Data_Centres). This is mostly a literature deck: every number is cited on the slide to a paper, standard, MSA or datasheet with a link, or computed live by the deck's interactive; vendor claims are labelled as the vendor's own and illustrative coefficients as illustrative. Device physics is explained in [Silicon Photonics Fundamentals](https://github.com/BrendanJamesLynskey/Optical_Hub_Silicon_Photonics) and linked, not repeated. Every concept used here is explained, with links, in the [series glossary](https://brendanjameslynskey.github.io/Optical_Hub_Data_Centres/#glossary).
